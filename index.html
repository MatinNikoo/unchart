Get the numbers back out of a chart.

Paste a screenshot and get the numbers back. Unchart reads the axes, traces every line, bar, slice and dot, puts an error bar on each number, says so when it is not sure, and tells you when a chart is built to mislead. Takes about 3 seconds, right in your browser.

Worked out from the table, so your edits change it.

A small neural net that finds the series instead of the color rules. It fixes some charts and breaks others, and on my test sets it was a wash, so it is off. Turning it on reads the picture again and loads a 3 MB file the first time.

01 ReadRead the labels
It spots the glyphs itself and reads them in one pass, so tiny tick numbers and gray text still come through.

    
02 FitFit the axes
A fit that ignores outliers lines up the tick values, skips misreads, and catches log axes and lost minus signs.

    
03 TraceTrace the data
Each color is a series. Lines snap back to their real data points. Bars and dots get measured.

    
04 CheckCheck the chart
Bars that skip zero, two y axes, log scales and zoomed axes, with what each does to your read.
